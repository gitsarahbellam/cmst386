Privacy Policy

This page explains how Reach San Pedro may collect, use, and protect information provided through this website.

YOUR PRIVACY

Our Commitment

Reach San Pedro respects the privacy of people who visit this website. This policy describes the types of information that may be collected through the website and how that information may be used.

Collection of Information

Reach San Pedro may collect information that visitors voluntarily provide through forms on the website. This may include a name, email address, message, prayer request, or information about how someone would like to connect with the church. Basic technical information may also be collected automatically when someone visits a website, depending on the services used to operate and maintain the site.

Usage of Information

Information submitted through this website may be used to respond to questions, prayer requests, ministry inquiries, visit requests, or other messages sent to Reach San Pedro. Information may also be used to provide requested communication and to help the church understand how to improve its website and online communication. Reach San Pedro does not use submitted information for purposes unrelated to the request without appropriate reason or permission.

Third-Party Information Usage

This website may use third-party services to provide features such as maps, online giving, fonts, icons, website hosting, or other services. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. Reach San Pedro does not control the privacy practices of third-party websites or services. Visitors should review the privacy information provided by those services when using their features.

Cookie Policy

This website may use cookies or similar technologies when required by the website or third-party services used to provide certain features. Cookies are small files that may be stored on a visitor's device. They can help websites remember information, support website functionality, or provide information about website use. Visitors can manage cookie settings through their web browser. Disabling certain cookies may affect the operation of some website features.

Contact Us About Privacy

If you have questions about this privacy policy or about information submitted through the Reach San Pedro website, please contact us through the website's contact page. This policy may be updated when website features or information practices change.
