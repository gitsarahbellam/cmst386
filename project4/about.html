About Reach San Pedro

WHO WE ARE

About Us

Reach San Pedro is a church community focused on helping people grow in their relationship with God through worship, biblical teaching, connection, prayer, service, and outreach.

Reach San Pedro is part of the Reach Network and is committed to building a church community on a biblical foundation in Christ. The church seeks to develop disciples who grow in their faith, serve others, and engage with the San Pedro community.

The church's purpose is centered on helping people become fully devoted followers of Christ and experience restoration and new life in Him.

OUR FOUNDATION

What We Believe

Our Vision

Our vision is to reach, restore, and release. Reach San Pedro seeks to reach people with the message of Christ, help restore people to a relationship with God, and release them to fulfill their God-given purpose.

Biblical Foundation

Our faith and ministry are grounded in Christ and guided by biblical teaching. These foundations shape the way we worship, grow as disciples, serve others, and engage with our community.

OUR LEADERSHIP

Leadership

Reach San Pedro is led by a team committed to serving the church and its community. Hector and Vanessa Briseño are identified as points of contact for the church.

Leadership at Reach San Pedro supports the church's mission through teaching, ministry, discipleship, service, and care for the congregation and community.
