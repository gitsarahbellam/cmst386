GIVE

Give With Purpose

Generosity is one way we participate in the work of the church and support opportunities to worship, serve, connect, and reach our community.

WHY WE GIVE

Generosity Makes a Difference

Giving helps support the ministry and mission of Reach San Pedro. Your generosity helps provide opportunities for worship, ministry, community outreach, and service.

Support Ministry

Your generosity helps support the ongoing ministry of the church and creates opportunities for people to connect and grow in faith.

Serve Our Community

Giving helps provide resources for serving others and supporting outreach opportunities within our local community.

Build Community

Generosity helps create spaces and opportunities where people can gather, build relationships, and experience meaningful community.
