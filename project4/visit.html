PLAN YOUR VISIT

We'd Love to Welcome You

Visiting a church for the first time can feel unfamiliar, but we want your experience at Reach San Pedro to be welcoming and comfortable. Here's what you can expect when you join us on a Sunday.

SERVICE TIMES

Sunday Worship

10:00 AM

Join us for contemporary worship, practical teaching, and prayer. Services last approximately 75 minutes.

LOCATION

Reach Church San Pedro

136 S. Gaffey St.
San Pedro, CA 90731

Plan to arrive about 15 minutes early to allow time for parking and getting settled.

WHAT TO EXPECT

Your First Visit

Welcoming Environment

Our welcome team is ready to greet you when you arrive and help you find your way around.

Praise and Worship

Join us for contemporary worship as we gather together to praise God.

Biblical Teaching

Hear practical teaching grounded in biblical truth and designed to encourage spiritual growth.

Connection and Community

Connect with others, meet our church community, and discover opportunities to become involved.

Families and Children

A children's service is available on Sunday, providing an opportunity for children to participate in a church experience designed for them.

What to Wear

Come as you are. There is no need to dress up. Wear whatever makes you comfortable.

Parking

Arrive about 15 minutes early to allow time for parking and getting settled before the service.

AFTER SERVICE

Stay and Connect

After the service, refreshments are available in the lobby. It's a great opportunity to meet members of the pastoral team, connect with others, and learn more about the Reach San Pedro church family.
