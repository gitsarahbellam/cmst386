OUTREACH

Serving Our Community

We believe the church is called to care for the community around us. Through outreach, we look for practical ways to serve others, build relationships, and share God's love.

OUR COMMUNITY

Faith in Action

Outreach gives us opportunities to put our faith into action. By serving others and building genuine relationships, we can be a positive presence in the San Pedro community.

Serving Others

We seek opportunities to meet practical needs and encourage people through acts of kindness and service. Every opportunity to help can make a meaningful difference.

Building Relationships

Outreach is about more than completing a project. It is also about getting to know people, listening to their stories, and building relationships within our local community.

Sharing God's Love

Through practical service and genuine care, we want our community to experience the love and hope that are at the heart of our faith.

GET INVOLVED

Ways to Participate

Outreach can take many forms. Whether you have time to volunteer, want to support a community effort, or simply want to learn more, there is a place to begin.

Volunteer Your Time

Join a service opportunity and work alongside others from our church community.

Support Community Efforts

Help support outreach opportunities that provide practical assistance and encouragement to others.

Invite Others to Connect

Help people discover a welcoming church community where they can find connection, encouragement, and opportunities to grow.

TAKE THE NEXT STEP

Get Involved in Outreach

If you would like to learn more about outreach opportunities at Reach San Pedro, contact us and let us know that you are interested in serving our community.
